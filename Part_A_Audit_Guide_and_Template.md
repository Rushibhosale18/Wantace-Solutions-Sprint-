# Part A: Real Business Audit Guide & 1-Page Deliverable Template

## 1. Quick Selection Strategy for Candidate (Pick in 15 mins)
To ensure compliance with the *"makes, processes, or repairs products"* rule (resale shops strictly disqualified), pick any of the following accessible local units:
1. **Commercial Bakery / Confectionery Unit** (Flour & butter → dough → baking → packaging → dispatch to local cafes/retailers).
2. **Custom Tailoring / Uniform Manufacturing Unit** (Fabric rolls → cutting master → stitching operators → buttoning/ironing → school/corporate dispatch).
3. **Offset / Digital Printing Press** (Paper reams → pre-press plates → printing machine → lamination/die-cutting → binding → client delivery).
4. **Corrugated Box / Packaging Unit** (Kraft paper reels → corrugation fluting → gluing → slotting → bundle dispatch).
5. **Sheet Metal / Fabrication / Lathe Machine Workshop** (Raw steel bars/sheets → shearing/turning → welding → powder coating → assembly).

## 2. 20-Minute Owner Interview Questionnaire
Use these exact open-ended questions during your phone or in-person interview:
1. *"Monthly/Daily kitne orders aate hain aur average ticket size kya rehta hai?"*
2. *"Jab naya order aata hai, toh pehla entry kahan hota hai? (Rough dairy, WhatsApp group, register, ya seedha karigar ko bolte hain?)"*
3. *"Order aane se dispatch tak kaun kaun se hatho se order guzarta hai?"*
4. *"Aapko kaise pata chalta hai ki kaunsa order late ho raha hai aur kis vajah se ruka hai?"*
5. *"Agar koi raw material shortage ho ya karigar gair-hazir ho, toh customer ko delivery commit kaun aur kaise karta hai?"*
6. *"Damage, rejection, ya customer return aane par loss kiska hota hai aur record kahan karte ho?"*
7. *"Pichle 1 mahine mein sabse bada jhanjhat ya paisa/samay ka nuksaan kis wajah se hua?"*

## 3. 1-Page Official Deliverable Template (Ready to Fill / Submit)

```markdown
# 1-Page Operational Audit Report: [Business Name]

### 1. Business Profile
* **Business Name:** [e.g., Mahadev Packaging & Box Works / Royal Garments]
* **Location / Industrial Area:** [e.g., Wazirpur Industrial Area, New Delhi / MIDC Bhosari, Pune]
* **Product Line:** [e.g., 3-Ply & 5-Ply Corrugated Shipping Boxes for E-Commerce Sellers]
* **Scale / Volume:** ~45–60 customized orders per month; avg order size 2,500 boxes (~₹1.8 Lakhs/month turnover).
* **Team Size:** 1 Owner, 1 Floor Supervisor, 6 Machine Operators, 1 Delivery Van Driver.

### 2. Current Workflow Map
Customer Enquiry (WhatsApp specs & dimension) 
  → Owner notes on desk notepad & sends rough quote 
  → Customer confirms on WhatsApp (no formal PO) 
  → Supervisor checks paper kraft reels in stock 
  → Corrugation & slotting cutting machine setup 
  → Manual stitching & bundling (strapping tape) 
  → Loading in delivery tempo 
  → Cash/UPI upon delivery or 15-day credit slip.

### 3. Tracking Tools Used
* Physical 200-page red hardbound ledger (*Bahi Khata*) for credit balances.
* Laminated white-board next to die-cutting machine for daily production targets (wiped off daily).
* 1 WhatsApp broadcast list + individual chats for customer orders and delivery updates.

### 4. Main Operational Problem
**Order Dimension & GSM Miscommunication Leading to Rejections:**
Because carton dimensions (Inner vs Outer) and GSM paper strength requirements are taken verbally or over unorganized WhatsApp voice notes, machine operators frequently cut the wrong flute die or run out of specific 120 GSM paper mid-run.

### 5. Quantified Business Impact
* **Rejections/Rework:** 3 to 4 batches rejected per month (~₹18,000 to ₹25,000 in raw paper scrap & wasted electricity).
* **Owner's Lost Time:** Owner spends 2.5 hours every day physically standing by the slotting machine double-checking measurements before bulk cutting.
* **Cashflow Delay:** Deliveries delayed by 4+ days result in clients holding 100% of payment for 30+ days.

### 6. Direct Owner Quotes (Recorded in Original Language)
* **Quote 1 (On floor friction):**
  > *"WhatsApp pe customer bolta hai 10x8x6 inch inner size, karigar outer samajh ke cut kar deta hai. Jab tak customer se complaint aati hai, 1,000 dabba ban chuka hota hai aur paper bekar ho jata hai."*
* **Quote 2 (On tracking anxiety):**
  > *"Mera aadha din bas yeh poochne mein nikal jata hai ki Sharma ji ke 500 dibbe tempo mein load hue ya nahi. Jab tak driver se phone pe baat na ho, darr rehta hai."*

### 7. Evidence & Artifacts Attached
* [Photo 1: The laminated whiteboard where job orders are scribbled and wiped off daily]
* [Photo 2: The physical order slip / red register showing overlapping scribbled order entries]
* [Diagram 1: As-Is Process Flowchart showing handoffs and failure gates]
```
